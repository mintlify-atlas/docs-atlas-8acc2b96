The Scholarships & Fundings section of Shape Your Life ID helps you discover both study-abroad and local scholarship opportunities in one place. You can narrow down results using filters for country, funding type, degree level, and category, then read through each scholarship's full details — from eligibility period to researcher information — directly on the platform.

## Browsing scholarships

The main listing page is available at `/scholarship-and-fundings`. At the top of the page, up to four **highlighted** scholarships are displayed as featured cards, giving you quick access to the most prominent opportunities. Below the highlights, all scholarships are listed in a paginated grid showing **6 items per page**.

A filter sidebar lets you narrow results across four dimensions:

  <Card title="Funding type" icon="money-bill">

  <Card title="Degree level" icon="graduation-cap">

  </Card>

  </Card>

<Note>
  Highlighted scholarships always appear at the top of the listing page, regardless of the active filters.
</Note>

## Scholarship detail page

Each scholarship has its own detail page accessible at `/scholarship/{slug}`. The page surfaces all the information you need to evaluate an opportunity:

- **Title and subtitle** — the scholarship name and a supporting tagline
- **Country** — the destination or sponsoring country
- **Funding type** — e.g. fully funded, partial grant
- **Degree level** — the academic levels this scholarship covers (a scholarship can cover multiple levels)
- **Researcher** — the person or organisation associated with the scholarship
- **Application period** — `time_start` and `time_end` displayed as a formatted date range
- **Content** — the full description, requirements, and instructions

If you navigate to a legacy URL that uses a numeric ID (`/scholarship/{id}`), the platform automatically redirects you to the canonical slug-based URL.

## Scholarship categories and degree levels

Scholarships are organised into **categories** (stored in `scholarship_categories`) that describe the funding arrangement — for example, fully funded, partial, or self-funded. Each category groups related scholarships so you can browse within a specific funding model.

**Degree levels** are managed separately and linked through a many-to-many relationship, meaning a single scholarship can be tagged with multiple levels (e.g. both Master's and PhD). Degree levels are also available as a filter on the listing page via the `degree_label` field.
