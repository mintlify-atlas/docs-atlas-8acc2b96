Shape Your Life ID (SYL) is a community platform built for Indonesian youth, connecting members with curated scholarship opportunities, mentorship programs, bootcamps, events, and webinars. Whether you are exploring study abroad funding or looking to grow through structured learning, SYL brings everything together in one place.
